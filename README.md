# 🏦 Bank Marketing Campaign Funnel & Conversion Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** built to analyze bank marketing campaigns and customer subscription behavior. The dashboard provides valuable business insights into campaign performance, customer profiles, conversion rates, communication channels, and customer engagement.

It helps identify factors influencing customer subscription decisions and evaluates the effectiveness of marketing campaigns through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall bank marketing campaign performance.
- Evaluate customer subscription and conversion rates.
- Identify customer segments with higher subscription potential.
- Analyze campaign effectiveness across different communication channels.
- Understand the relationship between call duration and customer conversion.
- Explore customer behavior based on age, job, education, and marital status.
- Identify factors influencing customer subscription decisions.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve campaign effectiveness.
